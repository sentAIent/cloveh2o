Science-Backed Benefits

What cloves
actually do for you

Cloves have been used in traditional medicine for thousands of years — and modern science is confirming why. Here's what research shows about the active compounds in every bottle of CloveH₂O.

Meet Eugenol — the hero compound

Eugenol is cloves' primary active ingredient, making up 45–90% of clove essential oil. It's classified as Generally Recognized As Safe (GRAS) by the FDA and has been studied extensively for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Every bottle of CloveH₂O delivers eugenol naturally through our whole-clove cold infusion.

Extraordinary Antioxidant Power

Cloves rank among the highest antioxidant capacity of any food ingredient, with an ORAC score exceeding 290,000 µmol TE per 100g — outperforming cinnamon, oregano, and blueberries. Eugenol neutralizes free radicals, protecting cells from oxidative stress.

PMC Antioxidant Study, 2023
🔥

Natural Anti-Inflammatory

Eugenol inhibits key inflammatory pathways including NF-κB and suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α) — the same biological pathways targeted by many anti-inflammatory medications, but naturally and gently.

ACS Omega / NCBI, 2025
🦷

Oral Health Support

Research shows clove compounds effectively reduce oral bacteria like Streptococcus mutans — inhibiting plaque, gingivitis, and harmful bacteria. Herbal rinses with clove outperformed commercial essential oil mouthwashes in clinical studies.

Medical News Today / NIH
🌿

Digestive Comfort

Cloves stimulate the production of digestive enzymes and have recognized carminative properties — reducing gas and bloating. Studies also suggest clove extract may enhance stomach lining mucus production, supporting gut comfort.

MedicineNet / Traditional Medicine Research
🩸

Blood Sugar Balance

Animal studies show clove extract and its compound nigericin reduced insulin resistance in muscle cells, improved glucose tolerance, and enhanced insulin secretion. Early research suggests cloves may help maintain healthy blood sugar levels.

Healthline / PMC Animal Studies
🦠

Antimicrobial Defense

Laboratory studies confirm clove's eugenol, gallic acid, and tannins have powerful antimicrobial effects — with documented activity against bacteria including resistant Helicobacter pylori strains and urinary tract infection pathogens.

NCBI / Tropical Life Sciences Research
